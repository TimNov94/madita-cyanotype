Behind The Craft

Our Story & Solar Process

Founded in Bern, Madita stems from a deep reverence for the quiet majesty of the Swiss landscape. Using the 19th-century cyanotype technique, we coat heavy French and German rag papers with sunlight-sensitive iron solutions, placing hand-foraged alpine flora under the intense UV rays of the Swiss sun.

Because weather and sun intensities shift daily in the mountains, no two original prints develop identically. The resulting deep Prussian blue shades (#1B365D) hold the essence of a single sunlit moment in the Bernese Oberland.

01

Botanical Foraging

Carefully selecting wild ferns, pine needles, and alpine blooms across the Bernese Oberland and Lake Thun trailways.

02

Solar UV Exposure

Sensitizing archival Hahnemühle cotton paper with chemical emulsion and exposing under natural sunlight.

03

Alpine Water Rinsing

Washing away unexposed iron salts in clear water to reveal sharp indigo silhouettes and Prussian Blue depth.

Shipping & Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about delivery, framing, and artwork care

All original cyanotypes and fine art prints are carefully wrapped in acid-free glassine paper and shipped flat in heavy protective cardboard packaging (or sturdy reinforced tubes for A2 prints). We ship via Swiss Post priority with full insurance.
To preserve artwork integrity during transit, our pieces are sold unframed. However, all our works are created in standard European sizes (30x40 cm, A4, A3, A2) so you can easily fit them into off-the-shelf oak or walnut frames from your local framer or standard stores.
Cyanotypes are remarkably durable, but like any fine art piece, avoid hanging them in direct intense sunlight. If exposed to light over time and slight fading occurs, placing the artwork in a dark room for a few days naturally regenerates the rich Prussian Blue color!
